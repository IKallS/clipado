Clipado

Short-form highlight clips of gaming streams and podcasts, published on the Clipado YouTube channel.

What this app does. Clipado is a private, single-operator tool. It prepares vertical clips from content whose creators run public clipping campaigns, and a human reviews and approves every clip before anything is published. Approved clips are uploaded to the operator's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

The app only requests permission to upload videos (youtube.upload). It does not read, modify or delete any other data in your Google or YouTube account, and it is not offered to the public.

Clipado uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
