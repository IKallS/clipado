Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Clipado ("the app") is a private tool operated by a single person to publish approved video clips to their own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

  • An OAuth refresh token for the authorized YouTube channel, with the single scope youtube.upload.
  • The video files, titles and descriptions of clips the operator approved for upload.

The app does not access, collect or store any other Google account data, viewer data, comments, analytics or personal information of third parties.

How data is used and stored

The token is stored only on the operator's own computer, in a local file readable only by the operator's user account. It is used solely to upload approved videos to the operator's channel. It is never sold, shared with third parties, sent to other servers, or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Local copies of uploaded clips are deleted automatically a few days after upload. The token is kept until access is revoked. You can revoke the app's access at any time at Google security settings; the operator deletes the local token file when access is no longer needed.
