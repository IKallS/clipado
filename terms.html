Terms of Service

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Clipado is a private tool used only by its operator to publish approved clips to the operator's own YouTube channel. It is not offered as a service to the public.

YouTube

Use of the app is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Content

Clips are made only from content whose creators run public clipping campaigns that allow it, following each campaign's rules. Every clip is reviewed and approved by a human before upload. Rights holders who believe a clip should be removed can open an issue on GitHub and it will be taken down.

No warranty

The app is provided as is, without warranties of any kind.
